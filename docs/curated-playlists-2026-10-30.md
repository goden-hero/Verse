## Research notes

Mood and sequencing choices are editorial judgments from the available recordings and metadata, not claims that every song belongs to one narrow genre. Screen-title and album credits were checked against the local library; selected soundtrack relationships were additionally verified online. Fan tributes and covers are identified in playlist descriptions.

- [Blue Period official music page](https://blue-period.jp/music/): EVERBLUE and Replica are absent from this library, so no Blue Period soundtrack playlist was created.

- [Sony Music: LONGMAN spiral](https://www.sonymusic.co.jp/artist/longman/discography/AICL-4413): Mushoku Tensei II opening.
- [Mrs. GREEN APPLE official site](https://mrsgreenapple.com/news/detail/20423): Lilac and Oblivion Battery.
- [Frieren official site](https://frieren-anime.jp/news/1162/): Anytime Anywhere.

- [DreamWorks](https://www.dreamworks.com/movies/how-to-train-your-dragon): John Powell film credit.
- [Hidden Love album credit](https://music.apple.com/us/song/1692905939): Forever Star.
- [Love Game in Eastern Fantasy album](https://open.spotify.com/intl-ja/album/3UZK4M6vXd39dc8NxuevxV): drama soundtrack grouping.
