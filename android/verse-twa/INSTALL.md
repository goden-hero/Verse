# Install Verse on Android

1. Use an Android 7 or newer phone with an up-to-date TWA-capable browser (Chrome recommended).
2. Download **verse-1.0.0-release.apk** from the person sharing Verse with you.
3. Open the APK. If Android asks, temporarily allow **Install unknown apps** for the app opening the file, then choose **Install**. You can turn that permission off afterwards.
4. Open **Verse** from your app drawer. Log in with your Verse account, or tap **Create Account**. An existing login in the browser that opens Verse may already be available.
5. Start a song by tapping Play. Keep internet access available: your music comes from the hosted Verse server.

For updates, install the newer APK over the existing app. Keep Verse installed to retain its app setup; updates must come from the same signing key.

If a browser address bar appears, report it to the person sharing the app. The website association or browser support needs checking.

Media notifications, lock-screen controls and background playback depend on your browser, Android version and battery settings. Send your phone model, Android/browser versions and the steps that reproduce a problem.
